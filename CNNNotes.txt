Notes on current design:

BirdNET is a CNN which transforms a rolling 3 second snapshot into a spectogram. This spectogram is treated as an image and ran through a CNN which has 27 million parameters. Has an precision of .791 and an F0.5 Score of 0.414.

Notes on this solution
This solution does not isolate out the birdcall prior to image recognition. This is fine if sufficent data is available for training but under our usecase, a detection algorithm is likely required. They had 22,960 recordings to pull from

Our architecture steps:

1. Time based signal detection
2. Snapshot signal in time
3. produce spectogram (isolate signal in time frequency ideally). Must always be same length snapshot though
4. Include a ML algorithm for further classification?
